▸ THE TOOL

One terminal, one screen, the whole neighbourhood.

DEVICE SCAN

ARP-scan the local network and label every find — the router, your phone, that TV that never updates.

LIVE PINGS

Every device is pinged on a loop, colour-coded by latency. Green, amber, red — you can see them sweat.

PORT PROBE

OPERATIONS LOG

Every scan, every lag applied, every port found — timestamped and scrolling, the way it should be.

▸ THE LAG ENGINE

ARP spoof + traffic shaping. The polite version of turning someone's internet off.

Select a target, pick a delay, press enter. The engine reroutes their traffic through you and adds delay + jitter to everything they do. Press SPACE and they're restored, none the wiser.

  • ▸ Delay presets — 500ms to 5000ms (brutal)
  • ▸ Jitter presets — 100ms to 1000ms (chaotic)
  • ▸ Applies netem delay on the wire, not the app
  • ▸ Full cleanup on stop — forwarding off, shaping removed, spoof killed

It refuses to lag the gateway or yourself. It has standards.
